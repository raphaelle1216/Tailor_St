Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Tailor Street helps members of a school community browse donated uniform pieces, reserve available items, and view donation instructions. We keep the app simple and collect only what is needed to operate the uniform sharing program.

Information We Collect

Tailor Street does not ask public users for a name, email address, phone number, payment information, or precise location. When a user reserves an item, the app may store the reserved item, selected pickup time, pickup code, and any optional note the user chooses to enter.

Admin Information

Program organizers may upload uniform photos, item details, pickup slots, and reservation status updates so the app can show what is available and manage pickups.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate Tailor Street, including showing available uniform pieces, saving reservations, organizing pickup times, and managing donated inventory.

Tracking and Advertising

Tailor Street does not track users across apps or websites, does not sell personal information, and does not include third-party advertising.

Contact

Questions about Tailor Street or this privacy policy can be sent through the school program organizer.

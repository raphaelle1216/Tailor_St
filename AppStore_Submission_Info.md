## Description
Tailor Street helps students and families access donated school uniform pieces without stress or cost.

Browse available shirts, long skirts, collars, and sweaters, reserve an item anonymously, and choose a pickup time. Families can also view simple donation instructions for passing along clean uniform pieces that are ready for another student to wear.

Tailor Street is built for a school community: simple, private, and focused on making uniforms easier to share.

## Privacy Notes
The app does not ask users for name, email, phone number, payment details, or location.

Users can reserve a uniform and may leave an optional pickup note. Admins can upload uniform photos and manage pickup slots.

If Apple asks whether data is collected, answer based on the live app behavior:
- Optional user note/reservation info: collected only to manage uniform pickup.
- Photos: only uploaded by the admin for uniform listings.
- No tracking.
- No third-party advertising.
- No payment data.
- No precise location data.
